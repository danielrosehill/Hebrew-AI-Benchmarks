# Scope: Hebrew AI Benchmarks

Part of the [Hebrew AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI) index. This list covers **benchmarks, evaluation sets, eval suites and leaderboards** that measure how well AI handles modern Hebrew.

## In scope

- Leaderboards ranking models on Hebrew tasks (LLM, speech recognition, TTS, OCR, retrieval).
- Benchmark and held-out evaluation datasets in Hebrew, including English-Hebrew code-switching.
- Eval suites and harnesses built for Hebrew, and papers that introduce a Hebrew benchmark.
- Notable multilingual benchmarks with a Hebrew split, in a separate section, never mixed with the Hebrew-specific tables.

## Out of scope

- **Israeli AI in general.** Israel is the only country where modern Hebrew is an official language, so it is the natural centre of activity, and many entries here are Israeli. But an AI company, project or person that just happens to be based in Israel is out of scope: that list is far too large, and this set is about Hebrew-language AI specifically. Israel-focused AI is indexed separately in [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI).
- Training corpora with no evaluation role ([Hebrew-Datasets](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-Datasets)) and the models themselves ([Hebrew-AI-Models](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Models)).
- One-off model comparisons in blog posts or social media, unless they publish a reusable set or method.
